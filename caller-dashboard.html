Rows with a phone number already in the database are skipped (10-digit match; avoids duplicate scrapes). Results / city is capped at 300; values above 20 use extra API pages (may take ~30–60s per city on large counts).

What data does each Google Maps query return?

Google Maps search returns many fields; this app keeps what your team needs: company, phone, address, Google rating (stars & review count), website when present, notes for caller-only text, and your niche as category.

Rows without a phone are skipped so callers always have a number.

Opening Script

Cold Open

"Hi, is this [Owner Name]?

Great, this is [Your Name] calling from AllSorted Pro. We do bookkeeping specifically for small business owners, a lot of owners we talk to are spending 5 to 10 hours a month dealing with their own books.

We're offering the first month completely free just to show you what we do. Is that something worth a quick 15-minute conversation?"

If they say "what is this about?"

"Sure, we handle bookkeeping for business owners. We do everything in QuickBooks, reconciling your accounts, categorizing expenses, sending you a monthly report. Most owners save 6 to 8 hours a month. We're offering the first month free, does that sound like it could help you?"

Booking the Call

"I can send you a link right now to grab a 15-minute time slot that works for you. It's completely free, no pressure. What's a good email or number to send that to?"
Objection Handling
"I already have a bookkeeper / accountant"
"That's great, a lot of our clients have CPAs too. We actually make their job easier by delivering clean, organized books. Most CPAs love it. Is your bookkeeping fully caught up right now?"
"I do it myself"
"Totally get it, how many hours would you say it takes you each month? Most owners tell us 5 to 10 hours. That's time back in your day for $97 a month, and your first month is free anyway. Worth a 15-minute chat?"
"How much does it cost?"
"$97 a month for our founding member rate, that's locked in forever. And the first month is completely free. Most business owners spend more than that on software they barely use."
"Not interested"
"Totally fair. Can I just ask, is it that bookkeeping is handled, or more that the timing isn't right? Either way I won't bother you. Just want to understand."
"Send me an email"
"Absolutely. What's the best email? And just so I can personalize it, is bookkeeping something you're handling yourself right now or do you have someone?"
"I'm busy right now"
"Of course, when's a better time to call back? I'll put it in the calendar and reach out then."
