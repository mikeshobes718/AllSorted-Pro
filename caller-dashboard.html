▸ Self-serve tour Caller guide Explains every sidebar page, the four stat cards, the two panels below them, and what each lead status means. Expand on your first day or anytime you need a refresher. You shouldn’t have to ask a manager what a screen is for.

Use Overview as home base. Everything you need to dial, follow up, and get paid lives in the left menu.

Pages (left menu)

  • Overview — Snapshots for today and the week: call volume, bookings, show rate, commission, recent activity, and how many leads sit in each status.
  • Lead Queue — Your working list. Pull the shared team queue, add companies, work down the list, and set each lead’s status after every attempt.
  • Call Log — Full history of attempts and outcomes (voicemail, callback, booked, etc.). Use it to remember what happened on prior touches.
  • Appointments — Table of every logged meeting (newest first). Contact # shows visit 1 of N when the same phone comes back.
  • Script & Objections — Talk tracks and rebuttals—keep this handy while you’re on the phone.
  • Team assistant — AI helper for wording, questions, or quick coaching without leaving the portal.
  • Resources — Files and references the team shares (PDFs, guides, links).
  • Settings — Your profile and app options.
  • Sign out — Ends your session; use it on shared machines or when you’re finished for the day.
  • My commission (bottom of sidebar) — Showed or won appointments that were logged under your employee ID, at the rate on the card (needs Emp # on your account).

Numbers on this page

  • Calls Today — Dial attempts logged today. Log every try so activity stays accurate for you and the team.
  • Booked This Week — Appointments set during the current calendar week.
  • Show Rate — Of the meetings booked in scope, what share actually happened (showed). Higher means prospects are keeping commitments.
  • Commission Earned — Your payout only: showed or won appointments that were logged with your employee ID, at the rate under the number.

Panels below

  • Recent Call Activity — Latest outcomes; View all opens the full Call Log.
  • Upcoming Appointments — Next meetings; View all opens the Appointments page.

Lead status breakdown

  • New — Not worked yet (or reset to fresh). Start here when you sync the queue.
  • No Answer — You reached out but didn’t connect; try again or schedule a callback.
  • Callback — They asked you to call back at a better time—don’t lose these.
  • Booked — A meeting is on the books; follow up in Appointments as the date approaches.
  • Not Interested — Declined or disqualified; stops active pursuit unless something changes.
  • Called — You attempted contact; update to a clearer outcome (callback, booked, not interested, etc.) when you know it.

Status legend
  • Scheduled — On the calendar; the appointment has not happened yet.
  • Showed — They attended the sales appointment.
  • No Show — They did not attend or no-showed.
  • Won (paid + 2wk+) — Payment is in and they have completed at least two weeks of onboarding with us. Not for “the meeting went well” only.
  • Lost — They did not become a client after the appointment (opportunity closed lost).
  • Call log — Saving as Showed, No show, or Lost does not require a call log entry. If that contact is still on your lead queue, we may offer to open the call log so you can log a dial optionally.
  • Contact # (table) — Same phone shares one thread: Visit 2 of 3 means this is the second appointment on file for that number. If there is no phone, we match on name + company.

Scrape Google Maps into database

How it works: Pick a niche, then select a state or specific city. Set how many new leads you want per city — already scraped numbers are skipped automatically by matching phone numbers. The server tries multiple search variations (e.g. "trucking company in Buffalo", "trucking services in Buffalo") at different zoom levels to find as many listings as possible, paging through Google Maps until your target is hit or all results are exhausted. If the primary provider (ScrapingDog) can't reach your target, Apify's Google Maps crawler is used as a backup. Run multiple cities at once for speed. Progress is saved after every batch so nothing is lost on long runs. After the scrape finishes, counts are verified automatically. To get 1,000+ leads, target 50–100 per city and select "All Cities" in a state (or leave state blank for every indexed US metro — 650+ cities including expanded NY coverage).

What data does each Google Maps query return?

Google Maps search returns many fields; this app keeps what your team needs: company, phone, address, Google rating (stars & review count), website when present, notes for caller-only text, and your niche as category.

Rows without a phone are skipped so callers always have a number.

Opening Script

Cold Open

"Hi, is this [Owner Name]?

Great, this is [Your Name] calling from AllSorted Pro. We do bookkeeping specifically for small business owners, a lot of owners we talk to are spending 5 to 10 hours a month dealing with their own books.

We offer the first two weeks completely free — no credit card, no commitment — just to show you what we do. Is that something worth a quick 15-minute conversation?"

If they say "what is this about?"

"Sure, we handle bookkeeping for business owners. We do everything in QuickBooks, reconciling your accounts, categorizing expenses, sending you a monthly report. Most owners save 6 to 8 hours a month. Your first two weeks are completely free, no credit card — does that sound like it could help you?"

Booking the Call

"I can send you a link right now to grab a 15-minute time slot that works for you. It's completely free, no pressure. What's a good email or number to send that to?"
Objection Handling
"I already have a bookkeeper / accountant"
"That's great, a lot of our clients have CPAs too. We actually make their job easier by delivering clean, organized books. Most CPAs love it. Is your bookkeeping fully caught up right now?"
"I do it myself"
"Totally get it, how many hours would you say it takes you each month? Most owners tell us 5 to 10 hours. That's time back in your day. After the two-week free trial, it's $97 a month for three months while we prove our value, then $197 — still a fraction of a local bookkeeper. Worth a 15-minute chat?"
"How much does it cost?"
"First two weeks are completely free — no credit card, no commitment. After that it's $97 a month for the first three months while we prove our value, then $197 a month. That's still a fraction of what a local bookkeeper charges. Most owners spend more than that on software they barely use."
"Not interested"
"Totally fair. Can I just ask, is it that bookkeeping is handled, or more that the timing isn't right? Either way I won't bother you. Just want to understand."
"Send me an email"
"Absolutely. What's the best email? And just so I can personalize it, is bookkeeping something you're handling yourself right now or do you have someone?"
"I'm busy right now"
"Of course, when's a better time to call back? I'll put it in the calendar and reach out then."
